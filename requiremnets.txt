Professional services organizations such as IT consulting firms, engineering agencies and marketing groups depend on tracking time against explicit project scopes to stay profitable. Today, project data, time logs and invoicing live in disconnected tools. Team members log hours in third-party trackers, finance teams reconcile spreadsheets by hand for monthly invoicing, and project managers see budget consumption only after month end.
This project builds a unified Professional Services Automation (PSA) application on SAP BTP using the Cloud Application Programming (CAP) Model (Node.js) with a SAP Fiori (Fiori Elements) frontend, deployed as an MTA to Cloud Foundry. The solution models the real hierarchy of work: a Client has Projects; each Project has one Budget and is broken into Milestones and Tasks; Employees log hours against Tasks through Timesheets; approved hours drive real-time budget consumption and automated Invoices.
